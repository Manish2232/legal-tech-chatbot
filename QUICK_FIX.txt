# QUICK FIX - 3 STEPS

## 1️⃣  Sign up at Mailtrap (1 min)
   https://mailtrap.io → Click "Sign up"

## 2️⃣  Get your SMTP details
   - Integrations → SMTP
   - Copy Username and Password

## 3️⃣  Paste into .env file
   Edit `.env` and update these lines:
   
   SMTP_USER = "your-copied-username"
   SMTP_PASSWORD = "your-copied-password"

## ✅ Done!
   - Run: python test_email_config.py
   - You should see: ✅ Your email configuration is working!
   - Restart your app and signup will now get OTPs
